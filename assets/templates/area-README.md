## Shape

Two files always: `README.md` (what this area is, and its rules) and `STATE.md` (where it stands now).

Everything else appears only when it has something to hold: **`notes/`** with its first file, and **[`INDEX.md`](INDEX.md)** once there are about five. **`archive/`** appears here the first time something *inside* this area is retired, in a dated folder. (A whole area being retired goes to the root `archive/` instead.)

**`notes/` stays flat.** What a file is and how far it is trusted live in its header block, directly under the title (`kind:` and `trust:`), not in folders around it. Finding things happens through `INDEX.md` and plain search, not by clicking down a tree.

What is waiting on me here lives in the root `TASKS.md`, with this area's name on the card, never in a list of its own. **One list is the point**: a second list is where things go to be forgotten.

## The rule that makes this work

**The cap is across every branch, not per branch.** Five goals total, for a whole life. That is what forces the real decision — this quarter one area gets a slot and another does not. Give each branch its own goals and nothing ever gets chosen, only added.

**The measure is written with the goal.** A goal without one does not go on this list. Otherwise every review becomes an argument about what the goal meant, and the argument is always won by whoever is more tired.

## Rules

- **Quiet is not failure.** An area can be 🟢 with nothing open at all. Most of work is keeping things running.
- **⚪ quiet by choice is a decision** recorded in `DECISIONS.md`. **🔴 means nobody chose**: it just happened. Keeping those apart is the whole point of the column, and 🔴 means *"no information"*, not *"something bad happened"*. Say it that way.
- This board does not replace an area's `STATE.md`. It points at it.
- A new area gets its row **in the same action that creates it**. An area that is not on the board does not exist.
- **Ceiling: about 6KB, and every session pays for whatever is in it.** Every session reads this file before doing anything, so bloat here is charged again on every open. When it grows, what grew is almost always a dated paragraph, and its home is `DECISIONS.md`. **Move it; never summarise it into both places.**

## Rules

- **Maintenance is not failure.** A branch can be 🟢 with no goal at all. Most of a life is maintenance.
- **⚪ dormant is a deliberate choice** recorded in `DECISIONS.md`. **🔴 neglected means nobody chose** — it just happened. Keeping those apart is the whole point of the column.
- 🔴 means *"no information"*, not *"something bad happened."* Say it that way.
- This board does not replace a branch's `STATE.md`. It points at it.
- A new branch gets its row **in the same action that creates it**. A branch that is not on the board does not exist.

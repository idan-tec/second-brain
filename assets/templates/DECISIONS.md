# << "Decisions", in the owner's language >>

> **Append-only. An existing entry is never edited, and new entries go at the end of the file.** A reversed decision gets a new entry saying what it replaces, so the reversal stays visible.
> **Every decision lands here in the same turn it is said**: mine, and the ones made for me (by my manager, a client, a colleague), with who made it.
> **In their words.** The sentence that decided it, quoted, and whose sentence it is. A paraphrase reads, six months later, like a decision nobody made.
> **Nothing lives only here.** A decision that binds future work also goes where it is applied (the area's `README.md`, or the standing rules at the end of `CLAUDE.md`) and points back to its entry here by date and title.
> **When I am unavailable**, the agent takes the documented default, proceeds, and records it here as `assumed`; every `assumed` entry is confirmed or reversed at the next weekly review. A documented assumption can be corrected in a minute; a stalled system costs a week.
> Where this folder has no version control, this file is the entire memory of what was decided and why.

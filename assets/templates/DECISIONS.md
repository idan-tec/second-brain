# << "Decisions", in the owner's language >>

> **Append-only. An existing line is never edited.** A reversed decision gets a new line saying what it replaces, so the reversal stays visible.
> **Every decision lands here in the same turn it is said**: mine, and the ones made for me (by my manager, a client, a colleague), with who made it.
> **In their words.** The sentence that decided it, quoted. A paraphrase reads, six months later, like a decision nobody made.
> **Nothing lives only here.** A decision that binds future work also goes where it is applied (the area's `README.md`, or the standing rules at the end of `CLAUDE.md`) and points back to its line here.
> Where this folder has no version control, this file is the entire memory of what was decided and why.
